Who is doing this

Project DigiÉire is conducted at the J.E. Cairnes School of Business & Economics, University of Galway, and brings together climate science, agent-based social simulation, natural-language processing and wellbeing measurement, working with the communities and public bodies who will have to make the adaptation decisions.

The first phase builds the wellbeing instrument and calibrates it against Irish flood discourse; the second couples that instrument to an agent-based population model of Ireland; the third works with stakeholders to appraise adaptation proposals out to 2100.

We publish our methods, our figures and — within what data protection allows — our data. The application on this site is built from the same code as the research tooling, and every number it shows is checked against the research implementation before release.

A digital twin of Ireland

Project DigiÉireIrish Digital Twin for Flood‑Wellbeing by 2100

Climate change is accelerating glacier and ice-sheet melt, raising sea levels and intensifying heavy rainfall. Ireland’s Atlantic location and extensive coastline make it particularly vulnerable to coastal and inland flooding. DigiÉire is developing a digital twin of Ireland, underpinned by a dynamic agent-based model of its population, to explore how flood risks and adaptation choices could shape wellbeing through to 2100. The project places people’s quality of life at the centre of adaptation planning, looking beyond economic losses to understand how communities can thrive in a changing climate.
